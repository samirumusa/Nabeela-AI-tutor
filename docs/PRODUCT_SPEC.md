# Product Specification

## Vision
Nabeela teaches English to Hausa speakers through structured lessons and real conversation. It should feel like a patient teacher who uses Hausa strategically when that is the fastest route to understanding, then immediately returns the learner to English application.

## Core loop
**Curriculum -> Daily Mission -> 20-minute AI conversation -> evidence -> mastery update -> personalized next mission.**

## Learner experience
- CEFR progression A1-C2; initial product can focus on A1-B2.
- Speaking/listening are primary, with reading/writing support.
- Daily chunk: lesson content, vocabulary, collocations/chunks/patterns, listening/pronunciation, exercises, and a required speaking mission.
- Real-life scenarios: introductions, market, transport, work, interviews, hotel, restaurant, bank, hospital, presentations and customer support.
- Controls: `Explain in Hausa`, `Simpler English`, `Ask Nabeela`.

## 20-minute Daily Speaking Mission
Suggested structure:
- 2 min warm-up
- 3 min previous-day review
- 5 min today's topic
- 6 min role-play
- 3 min adaptive challenge
- 1 min wrap-up

Completion requires time **and meaningful participation**: sufficient learner speech, mission objectives, relevant responses and limited inactivity.

## Example teaching turn
Learner: `I want buy orange.`
Evidence: meaning understood; relevant response; vocabulary appropriate; article/plural weak; grammar partial; fluency/pronunciation separately assessed.
Tutor: `Good. You could say “I want to buy some oranges.” Now ask me how much they cost.`

## Hausa support
Every lesson supports normal English Mode plus Hausa Explanation Mode. Hausa support never reduces score by itself. Repeated Hausa replay of a concept is a reinforcement signal, not a punishment.

## Level progression
Course completion alone is insufficient. Promotion combines lesson requirements, required speaking missions, vocabulary/collocation/chunk/pattern mastery, grammar objectives, repeated multi-day evidence and a final level challenge.

## Boss challenges
Level-ending challenges use realistic tasks such as introducing oneself, shopping at a market, asking directions, describing yesterday and explaining future plans.
